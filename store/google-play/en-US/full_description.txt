Spin the shape and send every ball to the side of its color. A tap on the left, a tap on the right: easy to learn, impossible to put down.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap left or right to turn the shape by one side.
• Every ball has to land on the side of its color. Miss one and you lose a life.
• Every 10 balls you level up; every 3 levels the shape gains a side and gives you a life back: from the triangle all the way to the octagon.

COMBOS
Ten hits in a row and your points double, twenty-five and they triple, fifty and you're at x4. One miss and you start over.

SPECIAL BALLS
• Heart: one more life.
• Rainbow: fits any side and is worth three times the points.
• Hourglass: five seconds of slow motion.
• Chameleon: changes color at the last moment.

FOUR MODES
• Classic: five lives and the whole journey.
• 60 seconds: as many points as you can, a miss costs 3 seconds.
• Zen: no lives and no rush, just colors and music.
• Hardcore: one life and a fast pace from the start.

THE DAILY CHALLENGE
Every day a new sequence of balls, the same for every player, with a special rule. Unlimited tries, your best one counts. Keep your streak of days alive and share your result with a ready-made picture.

AWARDS, THEMES AND TRAILS
Eleven awards to win: each one unlocks a background theme or a trail for the balls.

FOR EVERYONE
Turn on symbols and every color gets its own shape, on the figure and inside the balls, so you can play even if you can't tell the colors apart.

AND MORE
• World leaderboards for every mode and for the daily challenge.
• Graphics, sounds and music generated in code: the soundtrack adds an instrument with every new shape.
• Works offline.
• No ads, no accounts, no purchases.
• In English and Italian.
